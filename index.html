About

Somewhere between a student and an artist.

I am a teenager who draws — constantly, quietly, mostly for themselves. Deep in the fundamentals: form, line, proportion. The kind of work that doesn't look like much until it suddenly does.

This portfolio isn't a highlight reel. It's a record — pieces I am proud of, moments where something clicked. I'd rather stay in the background and let the work speak.
